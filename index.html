Do we need to replace our EHR or billing system?

No. Klaropoint is designed to add structured validation, reconciliation, and documentation around existing clinic systems and workflows.

What does the free integrity review involve?

It starts with a conversation about your current workflow, where manual reconciliation happens, and which systems are involved. Do not send patient information or PHI through the public website form.

Timing depends on data sources, integration scope, security review, and clinic readiness. A focused initial assessment establishes the expected path and timeline.
